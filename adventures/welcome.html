Welcome to the adventure journal

Dr. TingPhysician, diver, traveler — in roughly that order on weekdays

This is the first entry, so it's the one that explains the rest. DocDiver is where my trips get written up: the dives, the cities in between, the long drives, the photos I'm proud of, the videos that mostly show bubbles, and the practical notes I wish someone had handed me before I booked.

How each adventure is written up

Every trip page has the same bones, so you always know where to look:

  • The facts box — where, how many dives, water temp and suit, visibility, operator, where I stayed.
  • The diving (or the trekking, or the driving) — what it was actually like.
  • Photos — tap any to open it big; arrow keys or swipe to move through.
  • Video — when there is one.
  • Above water — food, town, people, the rest day.
  • What I'd do differently — the honest part.
  • Practical notes — getting there, rough costs, what to bring.
